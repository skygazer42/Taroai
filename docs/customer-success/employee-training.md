# Employee Training

## Purpose

This training outline helps employees use Taroai safely for repeated business workflows. It is written for pilot users in workspaces seeded by a solution pack or custom skill.

## Chat And Task Console

- Start from the workspace chat and describe the business task, target artifact, and relevant IDs or files.
- Use concrete inputs such as ticket ID, account ID, SKU list, SOP ID, or reporting period.
- Watch the timeline for model planning, tool execution, approval pauses, browser captures, and artifact creation.

## Artifacts

- Treat artifacts as reviewable work products, not automatic source-of-truth changes.
- Download reports, cleaned spreadsheets, drafts, and summaries from the artifact panel.
- Share artifact links only with users who should have access to the underlying tenant data.

## Approvals

- Expect approval pauses before external messages, connector writes, price changes, CRM updates, escalations, or sensitive outputs.
- Add enough review context before approving or rejecting a gated action.
- Escalate uncertain approvals to the workspace admin or business owner.

## Sharing

- Share runs and artifacts through tenant-controlled sharing features instead of copying sensitive content into unmanaged tools.
- Use workspace-specific channels for review and avoid mixing customer, department, or regulated data.
- Confirm the recipient has the right tenant and workspace access before sharing.

## Feedback

- Submit feedback when an answer is wrong, slow, costly, missing a needed skill, or useful enough to repeat.
- Link feedback to the run, artifact, skill, solution pack, or onboarding step when possible.
- Do not include secrets, passwords, tokens, or private customer content in feedback comments.

## Safe Use

- Do not ask the agent to bypass approvals, access data outside your role, or send external messages without review.
- Do not paste credentials or unrestricted customer exports into chat.
- Check citations, source data, and artifact contents before using output in customer-facing or operational decisions.

## Practice Session

- Run one solution pack sample input.
- Review the timeline and identify the tool execution step.
- Approve or reject one gated action.
- Download one artifact.
- Submit one feedback item that describes whether the workflow should be repeated.
